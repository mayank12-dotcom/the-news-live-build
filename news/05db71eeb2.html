EU slaps anti-dumping duties on steel imports from India, 4 other countries

The European Union on Monday imposed definitive anti-dumping duties on imports of cold-rolled flat steel from India and four other countries, with rates ranging from 3% to 27.9%. This follows an investigation that found the products were being sold in the EU at dumped prices.
