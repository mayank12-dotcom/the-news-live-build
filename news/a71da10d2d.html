Logitech to Give All 7,000 Employees a Day Off to Play GTA 6

Logitech has announced a new initiative called Logi Game Day, which will allow approximately 7,000 employees worldwide to take a paid day off to play Grand Theft Auto VI, Tom's Hardware reports. The program is available to all company employees, including interns.
