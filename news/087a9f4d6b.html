Rajasthan to Run SAANS Campaign from November 12 to February 28 to Prevent Pneumonia in Children

Rajasthan will run its SAANS campaign from November 12 to February 28 to curb pneumonia among children under five through early detection, referrals and awareness. The state has paired the drive with National Newborn Week and told hospitals to keep medicines, diagnostics and protocols ready for the winter season.
