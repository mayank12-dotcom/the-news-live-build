Ahead of Budget 2027, Sebi recommends removal of 12.5% LTCG tax imposed on foreign investors | Latest update

SEBI has proposed scrapping LTCG tax on foreign equity investments and offering tax relief to domestic mutual fund investors switching schemes. The recommendations have been submitted ahead of Budget 2027 and await government approval.
