Supreme Court declines Tamil Nadu's plea seeking 11 TMC Cauvery water from Karnataka

The Supreme Court on Friday declined to entertain Tamil Nadu's petition seeking a direction to Karnataka to release 11 thousand million cubic feet (TMC) of Cauvery water to make up for the alleged shortfall in its share.
