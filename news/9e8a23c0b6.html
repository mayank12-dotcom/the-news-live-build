Mother Dairy milk gets costlier: Dairy giant hikes rate by Rs 2-4 per litre from Thursday; check prices

Milk is set to get costlier in Delhi NCR from November 12, with dairy companies Mother Dairy and Amul increasing prices by Rs 2 to Rs 6 per litre. The price hike comes amid lower milk supplies and higher costs of buying milk from farmers.
