Nifty Bank hits new record high as old warhorse HDFC Bank powers 13% November rally

The Nifty Bank index hit a fresh record high, rising 13% in November as HDFC Bank shares surged 17%. Strong credit and deposit growth have supported banking sector fundamentals, but analysts caution that the sharp rally has left the index overbought.
