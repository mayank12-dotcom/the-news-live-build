OPPO Reno 17, Reno 17 Pro launched with up to 8,000mAh battery, 200MP camera: Price, specifications

OPPO has launched the Reno 17 and Reno 17 Pro in China, featuring up to 8,000mAh batteries, 50MP periscope telephoto cameras and up to a 200MP primary camera. Prices start at CNY 3,599 (around ₹51,000), while an India launch timeline is yet to be confirmed.
