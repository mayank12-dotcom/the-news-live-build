Bank collapse leaves half of Paraguay without funds, economist-president under house arrest

A bank serving nearly half of Paraguay's population has collapsed, leaving millions of customers struggling to access their savings and putting public funds at risk. President Santiago Pena, a former economist with the IMF, has been impeached and placed under house arrest.
