Sooraj Barjatya's 'Yeh Prem Mol Liya', starring Ayushmann Khurrana and Sharvari, gets a U/A certificate,

Sooraj Barjatya’s ‘Yeh Prem Mol Liya’, starring Ayushmann Khurrana and Sharvari, has been cleared by the Central Board of Film Certification. The family drama, which arrives in theatres on November 27, 2026, has received a U/A rating and now has a confirmed running time.
