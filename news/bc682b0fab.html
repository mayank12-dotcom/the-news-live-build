'King' song 'Daddy Cool' out now: Shah Rukh Khan and Kareena Kapoor Khan reunite after 'Ra.One'; SRK says

Shah Rukh Khan's much-awaited film 'King' has dropped its new track 'Daddy Cool', which reunites him with Kareena Kapoor Khan on screen for the first time since 'Ra.One' in 2011. The party number is a fresh take on a classic disco hit and promises a fun musical ride ahead of the film's December release.
