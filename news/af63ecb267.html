US judge strikes down Trump rule restricting stays for foreign students, journalists

A US federal judge on Monday struck down a Trump administration rule that sought to impose fixed periods of stay on foreign students, exchange visitors and international journalists, ruling that the policy was arbitrary and not in accordance with the law.
