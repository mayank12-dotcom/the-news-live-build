Committed to ensure all inconsistencies in voter lists are addressed: EC to SC

The Election Commission (EC) has told the Supreme Court that it remains committed to ensuring that all eligible voters find their names in electoral rolls while simultaneously addressing inconsistencies in the lists during the ongoing Special Intensive Revision (SIR) exercise.
