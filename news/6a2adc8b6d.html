ECI’s Internal Records of 2024 Elections: In 51 Seats of Two States and a UT, Votes Cast Do Not Match Votes Counted

In <b>Maharashtra, Haryana and J&K</b>, the ECI did not record the number of votes polled in some seats. In others, the votes counted from EVMs mismatched the votes cast by lakhs. We accessed ECI’s data which it is required to audit to ensure elections are not rigged.
