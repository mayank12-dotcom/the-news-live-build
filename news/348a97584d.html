Total Chess: Top scorers obtain 4/6 points on first day of group stage

The group stage of the Total Chess Pilot began in Budapest on Wednesday, with players contesting two games at a 45+30 time control. Six players finished the day with 4 points out of a possible 6: Fabiano Caruana, Alireza Firouzja (pictured), Maxime Vachier-Lagrave, Ian Nepomniachtchi, Jan-Krzysztof Duda and Le Quang Li
