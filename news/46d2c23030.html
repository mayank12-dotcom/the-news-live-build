Tamil Nadu CM Vijay approves 6 AM shows in the state, Diwali releases like Suriya's 'Scene' and Nayanthar

Theatre owners and moviegoers in Tamil Nadu have reason to celebrate as the state government has introduced a major change in film screening timings. The new order allows theatres to screen new Tamil movies from 6 AM for a week following their release.
